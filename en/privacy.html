Book of Days Privacy Policy

Updated November 14, 2026

Book of Days is an iPhone app made by Jingwen Fan, an independent developer. It doesn't collect any of your personal information. This page explains what data the app uses and where it's stored.

What you record

The days you add, including their names, dates, reminder settings, colors and background photos, are stored only on your iPhone.

If you sign in with your Apple Account, they sync through Apple's iCloud to your own iCloud account. Only you can access them; we can't see them.

Sign in with Apple

Signing in is optional and only turns on iCloud sync. Apple provides a user identifier that contains no personal information, plus the name you choose to share. Both stay on your iPhone and are used only to show your name and to tell whether sync is on. Book of Days doesn't get your email address.

Photos

You choose background photos with the system photo picker, so Book of Days can read only the photo you pick and can't access your library. The photo is compressed and saved with that day.

Calendar

Book of Days asks to read your calendars only when you tap Import from Calendar. Your calendars are read on your iPhone to list the events you can import. The events you choose are copied as days and saved like any other day. Book of Days never changes your calendars.

Notifications

Reminders are sent by your iPhone itself and never pass through a server.

What we don't do

  • We don't collect data or upload it to our servers (Book of Days has no servers)
  • No ads, and no third-party analytics or tracking
  • We don't share data with anyone

Deleting your data

  • Swipe left on a day in the list, or tap Delete while editing it
  • Deleting the Book of Days app deletes all of its data on your iPhone
  • Data in iCloud can be deleted in your iPhone's Settings, where you manage iCloud storage

Children

Book of Days is suitable for all ages and doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page and the date at the top will be updated.
