Book of Days Support

Remember every day that matters: anniversaries, countdowns and recurring reminders.

How do I add a day?

Tap + in the top-right corner, choose a type (Anniversary, Countdown or Recurring), enter a title and date, then tap Save.

How do I keep track of a lunar birthday?

Choose Anniversary, turn on Lunar Calendar, then pick the lunar date. Each year the app works out the next date on the lunar calendar.

How do I add a background photo?

While editing a day, tap Add Background Photo and choose a photo. You can then adjust the text position, text color and background blur.

How do I add a widget?

Touch and hold an empty area of the Home Screen, tap Edit in the top-left corner, choose Add Widget, search for Book of Days, then pick a size and add it. Lock Screen widgets are added while you customize the Lock Screen.

Why didn't I get a reminder?

Make sure notifications are allowed in Settings → Notifications → Book of Days, and that the day has a reminder set.

Will I lose my days when I change phones?

Sign in with your Apple Account in the app's settings, and your days sync to your own iCloud. Sign in to the same Apple Account and iCloud on your new phone, and they sync back automatically.
